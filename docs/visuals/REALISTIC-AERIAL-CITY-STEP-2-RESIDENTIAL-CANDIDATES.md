## Review requirements

- Confirm that facade materials, glazing, balconies, roof details and window repetition hold up at the intended drone camera distance.
- Inspect triangle count, mesh fragmentation, UVs, texture resolution, normal maps and material count in Blender.
- Normalize origin and orientation while preserving declared real-world dimensions.
- Create LODs and test shadow-map cost, texture memory and transparent-surface overdraw.
- Keep all candidates disabled in runtime until the original files are reviewed.
